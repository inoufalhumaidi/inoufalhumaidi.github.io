About

Curious by default, rigorous by habit.

I'm a data analyst who enjoys the full path from a messy CSV to a decision someone actually acts on. My work usually starts with a question a team can't answer yet, moves through SQL and Python to clean and model the data, and ends in a dashboard or report that makes the answer obvious — and easy to trust.

I care about reproducibility, clear visual design, and analyses that hold up when someone pokes at them. Outside of client work, I dig into open datasets to sharpen my statistics and visualization skills, and I write up findings so the reasoning is as visible as the result.
